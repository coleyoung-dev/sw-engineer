AR Store Scan helped users discover nearby stores or branded locations through mobile AR. I connected location, direction, service data, and embedded Unity behavior into one reusable client flow.

- Visualized nearby stores with GPS and compass data.
- Connected store API data to AR markers and store UI.
- Checked Android/iOS embedded app behavior.
- Structured the feature as a reusable HARS content module.
