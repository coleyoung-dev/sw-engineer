AR Portal was a HARS content type where users create a portal in real space and enter a virtual environment through the mobile camera view. I worked on the Unity client implementation around marker or tap-based portal creation, virtual spaces, object interaction, and connections to other AR content.

- Used ARFoundation Image Tracking and Stencil Buffer techniques to render a portal space naturally over the camera view.
- Designed the content flow so the portal could connect to 3D environments, object interaction, coupons, and minigames.
- Reworked image recognition toward TensorFlow Lite-based model inference to improve recognition-driven HARS content.
- Helped turn AR Portal into a reusable module for branded worlds, pop-up store concepts, and marketing experiences.
