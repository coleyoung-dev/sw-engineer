AR Portal was a reusable HARS content type that opened a virtual space through the mobile camera view. I worked on the Unity client flow for portal creation, recognition, rendering, and connected AR interactions.

- Rendered portal spaces over the camera view.
- Connected portal content with 3D spaces, objects, coupons, and minigames.
- Improved recognition-driven content quality.
- Helped shape AR Portal into a reusable marketing module.
